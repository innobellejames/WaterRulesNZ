ABOUT WATERRULES NZ

Meet your guide to
New Zealand water rules.

WaterRules NZ is a guide developed by Belle James to help New Zealand water suppliers find original requirements, local water pages and practical resources. It is an independent information site.

WaterRules NZFind · understand · plan · learn

Built for suppliers, operators, community teams and curious learners in Aotearoa New Zealand. Bring your question; leave with a clearer path to the original publication.

What you can do here

Find relevant requirements

Describe a supply question and explore source passages with linked references.

Understand the terminology

Look up water terms, abbreviations and their original sources.

Build your knowledge

Practise with ten-question attempts and share a completed learning result.

Plan the next step

Use sampling tools and official templates as starting points for your supply.

Explore your local context

Find council water pages, notices and map resources.

Read the source

Browse the Act, Standards, Rules, guidance and technical references.

How an answer takes shape

  1. You provide context. Your question and selected supply type help focus the search.
  2. The tool finds relevant passages. Automated matching and prepared overviews organise the curated source collection.
  3. You check the original. Read the references, version and scope before applying a requirement.

This site does not have a live regulator or officer behind the answer box. It does not certify compliance or determine whether a treatment design is suitable.

Why start here instead of a general Google search?

A general search is useful for discovery, but the result list does not by itself tell a supplier whether a page is current, legally binding, local guidance or historical commentary. WaterRules NZ organises those source roles around supplier questions and gives you a route back to the original text.

Find the right layer

Start with the Act, Standards, Rules or an applicable Acceptable Solution, then read regulator guidance for practice. Source labels help prevent an industry article or old PDF being mistaken for a current requirement.

See the evidence beside the answer

Focused answers show an overview table with linked references in the final column. Open each source and check its date, scope and exact wording.

Move to the right person

Council links help with local notices and assets; Taumata Arowai is the place to clarify unclear national supplier guidance. A search result cannot confirm your supply's compliance status.

This site is an automated navigation aid with a curated, incomplete collection of sources. Search engines may find newer or additional material. Verify current law and local facts before acting.

Books, technical manuals and research

Standard Methods for the Examination of Water and Wastewater · 24th edition

APHA · AWWA · WEF · Consensus laboratory methods for biological, chemical and physical water analysis. Publisher access requires a purchase or subscription; no unrestricted complete PDF is offered here.

WHO Guidelines for Drinking-water Quality · 2026

World Health Organization · Fourth edition with three addenda. International health and risk management guidance; use the New Zealand Standards for local legal limits.

WHO Water Safety Plans · 2026 chapter

World Health Organization · Risk assessment and management from catchment to consumer in the latest WHO Guidelines. Read alongside Taumata Arowai safety planning guidance.

WHO Guidelines for Drinking-water Quality: Small Water Supplies · 2024

World Health Organization · International guidance on proportionate risk management and sanitary inspection for smaller supplies. It does not replace New Zealand supply categories or rules.

Water Safety Plan Manual · 2nd edition (2023)

WHO · International Water Association · Practical steps and templates for developing and improving a water safety plan. The official publication page provides the full manual download.

Groundwater

R. Allan Freeze and John A. Cherry · Groundwater Project · Foundational hydrogeology textbook, freely available through its publisher. Technical background, not a New Zealand compliance instrument.

Drinking water safety and the Havelock North outbreak

Graham, Russell and Gilpin · Journal of Water and Health (2023) · Peer-reviewed analysis of water safety planning implementation after a serious waterborne outbreak. Use for learning, not as a binding rule.

Historical nitrate in NZ public drinking water supplies

Chambers et al. · Water Environment Research (2026) · Peer-reviewed national reconstruction of 2000–2024 nitrate measurements with imputed gaps. Population estimates are not a reading for any particular bore or zone.

Nitrate contamination in New Zealand domestic drinking water

Rogers et al. · Science of the Total Environment (2025) · Open access research on rural groundwater sourced self-supplies and nitrate; check the Standards for legal limits.

Drinking water quality and enteric disease: a nationwide case-crossover study

Chambers et al. · Journal of Exposure Science & Environmental Epidemiology (2026) · Peer-reviewed New Zealand epidemiology; an association study, not a supplier-specific risk determination.

Drinking Water Distribution Systems: Assessing and Reducing Risks

National Academies Press (2006) · Scientific reference on distribution system hazards and risk reduction. Interpret in the New Zealand regulatory context.
