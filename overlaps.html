RULE OVERLAPS

When more than one
water rule applies.

Some businesses must meet more than one set of water rules, for example a campground with a café, or a farm-stay on its own bore. This page shows what each set of rules says, in its own words.

Drinking water rules

Water Services Act 2021 · Ask Taumata Arowai

Food safety rules

Food Act 2014 and Food Regulations 2015 · Ask your food verifier or MPI

Campground rules

Camping-Grounds Regulations 1985 · Ask your council

Why the rules can seem to overlap or clash

These three pieces of official wording are worth reading side by side:

WaterRules does not decide how these fit together for your site. If you are unsure, ask Taumata Arowai and your food verifier or council.
