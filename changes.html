Effective 1 July 2027

The 2022 Rules (Revised 2024) remain in effect until then. This is a selected change review, not a complete rule crosswalk. The final instrument takes precedence over summaries; check each provision and its conditions.

Reporting transition dates

Regulator guidance gives 1 April 2027 for the 2026 full annual reporting round. Schedule 2 of the final 2026 Rules specifies 14 July 2027 for applicable June monthly reports, 28 July 2027 for applicable April–June quarterly reports, and 22 September 2027 for the listed January–June transition rules. These are separate reporting obligations.
