USER GUIDE

A clear path through
WaterRules NZ.

Start with a question, follow the evidence and move to the tool you need. This guide explains the navigation and everyday workflows.

Your first five minutes

  1. Choose a starting point. Use Ask for a question, About → Source Library for an original document or Dictionary for an unfamiliar word.
  2. Read the Terms of Use. On Ask, tick the agreement before submitting.
  3. Describe your supply. Add the water source, population, location and issue. Choose the closest supply type when known.
  4. Open the evidence. Read the linked document and confirm its version, scope and exceptions.
  5. Continue your task. Ask a follow-up, open a template, plan sampling or test your understanding in the quiz.

Find your way around

On a large screen, the left menu groups sections under Home, Ask & Learn, Plan & Review, Maps & Data, and About & Help. Use “Find a section” to narrow the list. On a phone, tap the menu icon at the top, choose a section and the menu closes.

The top shortcuts open Home, Ask, Review, Maps, Rain and this Guide. Your browser's Back and Forward buttons move between sections. Share this page opens device sharing, or copies the current section’s link when device sharing is unavailable.

Step-by-step workflows

Ask a useful question and check an answer
  1. Open Ask and agree to the Terms of Use.
  2. Type one clear question. For example: “What monitoring applies to our network serving 350 people?”
  3. Choose your supply type if known and select Find sources.
  4. Read the overview and references together. Source matching is automated; a passage may not settle your situation.
  5. Use Ask a follow-up to add context. For uncertainty about requirements, contact the regulator.
Open Ask
Learn with the quiz and share a result
  1. Open Knowledge quiz and choose a focus: mixed, scenarios, current Rules, upcoming Rules or Acceptable Solutions.
  2. Answer the ten questions and review explanations and cited pages.
  3. When complete, use Share result, Copy result, WhatsApp or the text download.
  4. For Facebook or LinkedIn, paste the copied score into your post. Start a new attempt for another selection.

A quiz score is a learning result, not a compliance assessment.

Open Knowledge quiz
Study a topic and download a learning record
  1. Open Learning Hub and choose a topic. Read the six lessons, explore worked examples and try the practice activities.
  2. Start the optional assessment and answer all 30 questions. At least 26 correct answers meets the 85% threshold.
  3. Review the score and recorded attempt date. Enter a display name and download the learning record.
  4. If automatic saving does not start, use the visible Save or open PDF link.

The record is self-assessed, unproctored and non-accredited. It does not verify identity, practical competence or regulatory compliance.

Open Learning Hub
Use a template or plan sampling
  1. Open Templates & safety plans and select a resource that matches your task.
  2. Check its publisher, version and supply eligibility; open the official source if needed.
  3. Download the file and adapt it to your supply.
  4. Use Sampling sched for planning, then confirm the actual frequencies, locations and reporting duties against current requirements.

Check rainfall

Open Weather & rainfall and select a region. Wellington and Canterbury readings load live from the councils. Southland and Otago do not let other websites read their data, so open their official rainfall pages. The laboratory register list is a daily copy of the official HineKōrako register. CSV analysis is available for your own data.

Open Weather & rainfall
Understand saved work, refresh and sharing

Opening the website without a section link takes you to Home · Portal & news. Refreshing a section link keeps that section and active form or quiz progress. Question-bank snapshots are stored in this browser. Clearing browser storage, using a private session or changing devices can remove that history. Keep exported copies of important work.

Share this page shares a section link; it does not share private drafts or grant access to a restricted site. Quiz-result sharing includes only the result text you choose to share.
