About these learning resources and records

These are original WaterRulesNZ introductions. Source links, practice choices and reflection prompts help you explore a topic; they do not replace training, site procedures or advice from a qualified practitioner.

The optional certificate is titled “Introductory Learning Completion Record”. It is self-assessed, unproctored and non-accredited. WaterRulesNZ is the issuer. Each PDF carries a branded watermark with its score, attempt date and https://waterrulesnz.com/ website address. The watermark is a visual identifier and does not independently verify authenticity; no national qualification title, NZQA level or credit, official seal, professional licence or verified-competence claim is used.
