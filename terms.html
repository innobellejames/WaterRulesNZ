TERMS OF USE · WATERRULES NZ

Use this guide
with care.

WaterRules NZ is an independent, automated question-answer and source navigation interface developed by Belle James. It is not Taumata Arowai, and you are not talking to a live regulatory officer.

1. Informational navigation only

Answers help you locate potentially relevant legislation, regulator guidance and other sources. They are automatically assembled from indexed passages and curated guidance, may omit facts or changes, and do not constitute legal or regulatory advice, an official interpretation, an approval, or a compliance determination.

2. Supplier responsibility

Final verification of the requirements that apply to a particular supply, and compliance with them, remain the sole legal responsibility of the supplier. Check the current law, instruments, supply classification, consent and regulator communications; keep appropriate records and obtain professional advice where needed. Using this site does not transfer or discharge any supplier duty.

3. Decisions and urgent matters

Do not rely on an answer alone to make treatment, monitoring, reporting, notification or public health decisions. For unclear supply-specific requirements, contact Taumata Arowai. If water is or may be unsafe, follow the applicable urgent notification and consumer protection requirements; do not wait for this site. For a current local notice, check the issuing supplier or council.

4. Wai, cultural heritage and kaitiakitanga

Information about wai, water sources, locations, sampling and communities can carry cultural heritage, whakapapa, mātauranga and other sensitivities. Before entering, sharing or reusing such information, consider who has authority to provide it, its origin and intended use, privacy and security, and the interests of mana whenua and relevant kaitiaki. Seek appropriate engagement and permissions where needed. Do not paste sensitive site, personal, cultural or security information into questions. These stewardship principles are distinct from the specific legal duties that apply to a supply.

5. Your questions, visitor statistics and feedback

When you ask a question, this site saves the question, answer snapshot and linked references in this browser’s local storage. Anyone using this browser profile may see them. You can delete individual entries or clear the bank; clearing browser data also removes them. Do not enter personal, security-sensitive or culturally sensitive information. The saved snapshot may become outdated and must be checked against current sources.

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. Analytics cookies are set only if you choose Accept in the cookie notice; if you decline, Google receives limited signals without cookies. To see what people look for, the words typed into Ask Water and the site’s search boxes are sent to Google Analytics, shortened to 100 characters, with email addresses and long numbers removed. Answers to “Was this page helpful?” are counted, and anything you send through the feedback form is collected by Google Forms. Do not enter personal, security-sensitive or culturally sensitive information. To change your cookie choice, open Feedback and choose Cookie settings.

6. Sources and changes

External links lead to their publishers, whose material and terms may change. Check publication dates, the current version and the full context of each source. Check uncertain or outdated requirements with the publisher.

7. Evidence attachments

Agentic AI solution reads files in your browser memory. It does not upload or store their contents. Laboratory names and branches are sent to the website service for public-register lookup. Confirm extracted rows against the original file; export important findings before leaving the page. Automated checks cover selected requirements and leave missing evidence and unverified accreditation unresolved.

8. Learning activities and records

WaterRulesNZ introductory lessons and assessments are original learning resources. Downloaded Introductory Learning Completion Records are self-assessed, unproctored and non-accredited. They do not award a qualification, NZQA level or credit, professional registration, operator authorisation, or verified practical competence. No regulator or training provider endorsement is claimed.

Records show the submitted quiz score and the attempt time reported by this browser, displayed in New Zealand time. Identity and device time are not independently verified, and there is no public verification register. The entered name is used locally to generate the PDF. Reading progress is stored in this browser and reflection notes in the current browser session; neither establishes assessed competence. Do not present a learning record as evidence of a qualification or compliance.

Relevant data stewardship guidance: Māori Data and AI guidance and Ngā Tikanga Paihere: Kaitiaki. For the current legal change concerning Te Mana o te Wai, see Taumata Arowai's legislation update.
