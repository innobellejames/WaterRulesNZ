council.

Find your council’s water page and check the issuing supplier for current notices, outages or local service information.

Find a live water notice
  1. Look up the drinking water supplier if you are unsure who operates the supply.
  2. Search the council below and open its direct water page or a dedicated notices link where available.
  3. Confirm the notice status with the issuing supplier. For urgent safety concerns, follow their advice and Taumata Arowai’s notice guidance.

Regional councils usually cover water resources rather than household supply notices. The direct notice links below are a curated subset, not a live national alert feed.
